Theory behind the game
Inverse kinematics
Three joints, one target point. Try hand angles, solve the other two joints with the law of cosines, keep the closest pose that hits nothing.
Collision detection
Points along each arm segment are checked against the grid. Corner-to-corner gaps count as closed.
Breadth-first search
States are explored in order of move count, so the first one at the flag is a shortest solution. That gives par and hints.
Pruning
Out-of-reach targets and already-seen states are skipped before the expensive arm check.
Adversarial search
The devil tries every move, re-solves the level for each, and picks the one that makes your best route longest. One step of minimax.

How to play

  1. Click a dot to grip it. The arm swings over and its old base becomes the new hand.
  2. Click a block to pick it up, then click empty space to drop it. Press T to turn a plank.
  3. Reach the flag in as few moves as you can. Par is the best solution plus a little slack.

Pieces

  1. Hatched can't be gripped.
  2. Cracked breaks after one use.
  3. Greasy blocks can be carried but not climbed. Anything set on one slides off.
  4. A plate holds its door open while a block sits on it.
  5. ◆ is optional and costs a move.
  6. In devil mode, after each of your moves the devil breaks a red stone or greases a block. It goes after whatever your best route needs.
